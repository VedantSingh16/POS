# Cashier quick card

## Till flow

1. Open the assigned register with the opening float.
2. Use `/` or F2 to focus search; fast barcode-wedge input ending in Enter adds an exact barcode match.
3. Use quick keys/category buttons, quantity controls, line notes and line discounts.
4. Press F4 to hold, F12 or Ctrl+Enter to pay, and Del to remove the last line.
5. Select cash, card, UPI or a split tender. Cash accepts a tendered amount and returns change after configured rounding.
6. If a discount requires approval, enter the manager PIN in the approval panel.
7. At close, count by denomination where possible and record paid-in, paid-out, drop or pickup movements with a reason.

All amounts are stored in paise and recalculated by the server at checkout. Never rely on the displayed client total as the accounting record.
